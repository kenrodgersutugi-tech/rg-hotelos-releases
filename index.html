Everything in one system

Every department, one set of books

Each department works on its own screens. The money all ends up in the same place, so the owner sees the whole hotel at a glance.

Reception and rooms

Room grid, bookings, walk-ins, check-in and check-out, guest bills, debtors and a night audit that posts every room night by itself.

Restaurant and kitchen

Waiters order from a phone. Tickets print at the kitchen and the bar on their own, or show on a kitchen screen. Bills can go to a guest's room.

Cashier and M-Pesa

Shifts with a counted float, cash and M-Pesa prompts. A payment counts only once Safaricom confirms it. Typed M-Pesa codes wait for finance to check them.

Finance and pay

Imprest from request to surrender, expenses, salaries, advances and pay sheets. Every approval is recorded.

Staff and clock-in

Each person signs in with their own username and PIN, and sees only their own job. Staff clock in at the reception tablet. Managers approve with their PIN.

Stock and housekeeping

Receive deliveries by the crate, count the store and follow stock used by recipes. Housekeeping sees rooms to clean and reports faults.

Reports

Revenue by department, money by method, occupancy, waiters, cashiers and stock. Download them as Excel or print them to PDF.

Backups and updates

Encrypted backups every 15 minutes, plus a daily copy to a USB drive and your own Google Drive. Signed updates install at night and undo themselves if anything goes wrong.

How it works

Up and running in an afternoon

Install on the office PC

Run the installer on a Windows 10 or 11 PC wired to your router. It runs as a Windows service, starts by itself and comes back after a power cut.

Set up the hotel

The setup wizard asks for your licence, hotel name and logo, an admin account, departments, rooms and printers.

Connect tablets and phones

Staff devices join over the hotel Wi-Fi and are approved once by a manager. There's nothing to install from an app store.

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC (8 GB RAM, SSD), wired to the router
  • A UPS for the PC and the router
  • The hotel Wi-Fi, with a fixed IP for the PC
  • 80 mm thermal printers for the kitchen, bar and receipts
  • A USB stick for backups
  • Internet for the licence step (a phone hotspot is fine)

Before you install

Download and install

Run the installer as administrator. Until RG Devs' code-signing certificate arrives, Windows SmartScreen may say "unknown publisher": click More info, then Run anyway.

Only the office PC needs the installer. Tablets and phones open RG HotelOS in their browser over the hotel Wi-Fi. Once installed, the PC updates itself with updates signed by RG Devs, only at night when nobody is working, and puts the previous version back by itself if anything goes wrong.
