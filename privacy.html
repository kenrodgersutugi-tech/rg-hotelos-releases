Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data RG Devs ("we", "us") handles in connection with RG HotelOS (the "Software"), the RG Devs licence and M-Pesa services that the Software uses, and this website. We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations.

1. The short version

  • The hotel's guest, staff and financial records stay on the hotel's own office PC. RG Devs does not receive them.
  • To check a licence, the Software sends us the licence key, a one-way code made from the computer's hardware, the computer's name, the app version and the product name. It never sends guests' details.
  • When you buy a licence by M-Pesa, Safaricom handles the payment. We keep only a masked phone number, the amount and the M-Pesa receipt.
  • The optional AI assistant is off unless the hotel turns it on with its own Google Gemini key. It then sends staff questions, and the hotel data needed to answer them, to Google under the hotel's own account, with phone numbers and ID numbers removed first.
  • We don't sell personal data, and this website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

2. Who is responsible for what

The hotel is the data controller for the personal data of its guests and staff that it records in the Software, such as names, phone numbers, ID or passport numbers, stays, bills and attendance. The hotel decides what to collect and why, and must meet its own obligations under the Data Protection Act. These include telling guests why ID is collected, keeping data secure and, where required, registering with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

RG Devs is the data controller for the limited data described in sections 4 to 8: licensing, licence payments, support and this website. If a hotel asks us to help with its system and we see its records while doing so, we act only as its processor, only on its instructions and only for that support.

3. Data inside the hotel's system

The Software runs on the hotel's office PC and on the hotel's local network. Guest and staff records are stored in a database on that PC and are not sent to RG Devs. The Software is built to protect them:

  • Guest ID and passport numbers are encrypted on the PC and shown in full only to staff whose role needs them.
  • Each staff member signs in with their own username and PIN. Sensitive actions need a manager's approval, and an audit log records who did what.
  • After a period the hotel chooses (7 years without a stay by default), guests' ID numbers, phone numbers, email addresses and vehicle details are erased automatically. Names stay on bills and receipts, which are financial records.
  • Backups are encrypted with a recovery key that only the hotel holds.

The assistant. The Software's Ask button answers questions from its built-in manual and the hotel's own data, on the PC, without the internet. If the hotel's admin chooses to turn on the AI assistant, the hotel enters its own Google Gemini API key. A staff member's question, the recent conversation and the records needed to answer it (for example guest names, room numbers, bookings or totals) are then sent to Google to produce the answer. Before anything is sent, the Software removes phone numbers, ID and passport numbers, email addresses, vehicle details, PINs and keys. The assistant can only look up what that staff member's role may see, and it cannot change anything: it suggests an action, and the staff member confirms it. This happens under the hotel's own agreement with Google, which acts for the hotel; RG Devs receives none of it. The hotel decides whether to use it and should tell staff about it. It can be turned off, and the key removed, at any time in Settings → Assistant.

4. Data our licence service receives

When the Software is activated, starts a trial or renews its licence, it contacts the RG Devs licence service and sends:

DataWhy
Licence key (if one is entered)To check the licence is valid
Machine code: a one-way code made from the computer's hardware IDs. The IDs themselves cannot be worked back out of it.To tie a licence to one computer, and to allow one free trial per computer
Computer name, app version, product name (RG HotelOS)To recognise the computer when a hotel asks for help, and to issue the right licence
Hotel or business name (on purchase, or when RG Devs issues a key)To show who the licence belongs to

The licence service also records events such as activations, renewals and refusals, with the time they happened, to prevent misuse and to support the hotel.

5. M-Pesa payments

Licence purchases. When you buy a plan in the Software, your M-Pesa phone number is sent to Safaricom to start the payment prompt on your phone. We store only a masked version of the number (for example 0712***678), the plan, the amount, the M-Pesa receipt number and the result, so we can confirm payments and answer questions about them. Safaricom processes the payment under its own privacy notice.

The hotel's own M-Pesa payments (guests paying the hotel). These use the hotel's own Daraja account. Safaricom's payment results pass through an RG Devs relay so that the hotel's PC can collect them without being reachable from the internet. The relay holds each result for at most 7 days. The hotel's Daraja keys are stored encrypted on the hotel's PC.

6. Updates and backups

  • Updates: the Software checks a release file on GitHub every few hours and downloads signed updates from there. GitHub, Inc. receives the PC's IP address when it does.
  • Google Drive backups, if the hotel turns them on: encrypted backup files are uploaded to the hotel's own Google account. The Software can see only the files it creates there, and RG Devs cannot see them at all.

7. Support requests

When you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive what you send: usually your name, phone or email address, hotel name and the machine code, and the content of your message. We use it to answer you and to manage your licence.

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operation (see GitHub's privacy statement). The page loads its font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. Downloads are served from GitHub.

9. Who we share data with

We share personal data only as needed to provide the services:

  • Safaricom PLC, for M-Pesa payments
  • Neon, which hosts our licence database and services
  • GitHub, which hosts downloads and this website
  • Authorities, where the law requires it
  • Google, only if a hotel turns on the AI assistant with its own key (section 3). This is the hotel's choice and runs under the hotel's own Google account, not through RG Devs.

Some of these providers store data outside Kenya, for example in the European Union. Where data leaves Kenya, we rely on safeguards allowed by the Data Protection Act, such as the provider's contractual and security commitments.

10. How long we keep data

  • Licence records and events: while the licence exists, and for up to 7 years after it ends (tax and dispute records).
  • Purchase records (masked phone, receipt, amount): 7 years, as financial records.
  • Newly issued licence keys waiting to be collected by the app: 7 days at most.
  • M-Pesa relay results: 7 days at most.
  • Support messages: as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 3 years after the last contact.

11. Security

Licence keys are stored as one-way hashes, so even we can't read a lost key back. Licences and updates are digitally signed. Connections use HTTPS, and access to our systems is limited to RG Devs. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll tell you and the ODPC as the law requires.

12. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you may ask to be informed about, access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its use, and ask for it in a portable form, within the limits the law allows. Guests and staff of a hotel should first contact the hotel, which controls their records. For data RG Devs holds, write to us. If you aren't satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

13. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we'll announce important changes on this website.
